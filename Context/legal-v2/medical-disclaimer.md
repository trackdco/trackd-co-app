Any calculation Trackd performs depends entirely on the figures you enter: if the inputs are wrong, the output will be wrong. Always independently verify anything that matters before relying on it.

The reconstitution calculator carries particular risk. It converts the figures you enter, such as powder mass, solvent volume, and target dose, into a concentration and a volume to draw. A mistake in any input, or a misread output, can produce a result that is wrong by a large margin and lead directly to a serious dosing error. Trackd does not check your inputs and cannot verify the product you are working with. Trackd does not measure, prepare, or administer any dose; you do all of that yourself, outside the app. The calculator is a digital arithmetic tool that shows its working, meaning the concentration and volume it derives and the arithmetic used to get there, so that you can check every step yourself. It is not a dosing instruction.

Before preparing, drawing, or administering any dose, independently recalculate the result yourself and confirm it against the physical product. Do not rely on the calculator's output alone.
